# 🎯 Topic 1 — Percentage Change

## 📌 Formula

$$\text{Percentage Change} = \frac{\text{Difference}}{\text{Original}} \times 100$$

## ⚡ Shortcut

> Difference ÷ Original → convert to fraction.

---

## ✏️ Question 1

The production of a company in 2022 was 480 units and in 2023 it increased to 600 units. Find the percentage increase.

### 🔍 Solution

**Step 1 — Difference:**
$$600 - 480 = 120$$

**Step 2 — Divide by original:**
$$\frac{120}{480}$$

**Step 3 — Simplify:**
$$\frac{120}{480} = \frac{1}{4}$$

**Step 4 — Convert:**
$$\frac{1}{4} = 25\%$$

### ✅ Answer = 25% increase

---

## ✏️ Question 2

The number of employees in a company decreased from 800 to 600. Find the percentage decrease.

### 🔍 Solution

**Step 1 — Difference:**
$$800 - 600 = 200$$

**Step 2 — Divide:**
$$\frac{200}{800}$$

**Step 3 — Simplify:**
$$\frac{2}{8} = \frac{1}{4}$$

**Step 4 — Convert:**
$$\frac{1}{4} = 25\%$$

### ✅ Answer = 25% decrease

---

## ✏️ Question 3

Sales of a product increased from 250 to 325. Find percentage increase.

### 🔍 Solution

**Step 1 — Difference:**
$$325 - 250 = 75$$

**Step 2 — Divide:**
$$\frac{75}{250}$$

**Step 3 — Divide by 25:**
$$\frac{3}{10}$$

**Step 4 — Convert:**
$$\frac{3}{10} = 30\%$$

### ✅ Answer = 30% increase

---

## ✏️ Question 4

A value changes from 720 to 648. Find percentage decrease.

# ⚖️ Topic 2 — Ratio Comparison

## ⚡ Shortcut

> Find HCF of both numbers → divide both → write simplified ratio.

---

## ✏️ Question 1

The sales of Company A and Company B are 540 and 720 respectively. Find the ratio of sales of A to B.

### 🔍 Solution

$$540 : 720$$

Divide both by 180:

$$\frac{540}{180} = 3 \qquad \frac{720}{180} = 4$$

### ✅ Answer = 3 : 4

---

## ✏️ Question 2

Number of boys = 450
Number of girls = 750
Find ratio boys : girls.

### 🔍 Solution

$$450 : 750$$

Divide by 150:

$$3 : 5$$

### ✅ Answer = 3 : 5

---

## ✏️ Question 3

Production of two factories is 840 and 1260. Find ratio.

### 🔍 Solution

$$840 : 1260$$

Divide by 420:

$$2 : 3$$

### ✅ Answer = 2 : 3

---

## ✏️ Question 4

Sales of A = 500
Sales of B = 400
By what ratio is A greater than B?

### 🔍 Solution

$$\frac{500}{400} = \frac{5}{4}$$

### ✅ Answer = 5 : 4

# 📊 Topic 5 — Average

## 📌 Formula

$$\text{Average} = \frac{\text{Sum of all values}}{\text{Count}}$$

## ⚡ Shortcut

> **AP series** → Average = Middle value
> **Pair method** → (First + Last) = (Second + Second Last) → use that sum

---

## ✏️ Question 1

Find average: 20, 30, 40, 50, 60

### 🔍 Solution

Middle number = **40**

### ✅ Answer = 40

---

## ✏️ Question 2

Find average: 100, 200, 300, 400, 500

### 🔍 Solution

Middle number = **300**

### ✅ Answer = 300

---

## ✏️ Question 3

Find average: 25, 35, 45, 55, 65

### 🔍 Solution

Middle = **45**

### ✅ Answer = 45

---

## ✏️ Question 4

Find average: 120, 140, 160, 180

### 🔍 Solution

$$120 + 180 = 300$$
$$140 + 160 = 300$$
$$\text{Total} = 600$$
$$\text{Average} = \frac{600}{4} = 150$$

### ✅ Answer = 150

---

## ✏️ Question 5

Find average: 100, 200, 250, 300, 350

### 🔍 Solution

$$\text{Average} = \frac{1200}{5} = 240$$

### ✅ Answer = 240

# 💰 Topic 6 — Profit & Loss DI

## 📌 Formulas

$$\text{Profit \%} = \frac{SP - CP}{CP} \times 100$$

$$\text{Loss \%} = \frac{CP - SP}{CP} \times 100$$

## ⚡ Shortcut

> Same as percentage change — simplify (SP − CP) / CP to a fraction.

---

## ✏️ Question 1

Cost price = 600 | Selling price = 750 | Find profit percentage.

### 🔍 Solution

**Profit:**
$$750 - 600 = 150$$

**Divide:**
$$\frac{150}{600} = \frac{1}{4} = 25\%$$

### ✅ Answer = 25% profit

---

## ✏️ Question 2

CP = 800 | SP = 720 | Find loss percentage.

### 🔍 Solution

**Loss:**
$$800 - 720 = 80$$

**Divide:**
$$\frac{80}{800} = \frac{1}{10} = 10\%$$

### ✅ Answer = 10% loss

---

## ✏️ Question 3

CP = 250 | SP = 300 | Find profit %.

### 🔍 Solution

**Profit:**
$$300 - 250 = 50$$

**Divide:**
$$\frac{50}{250} = \frac{1}{5} = 20\%$$

### ✅ Answer = 20% profit

---

## ✏️ Question 4

CP = 480 | SP = 600

### 🔍 Solution

**Profit:**
$$600 - 480 = 120$$

**Divide:**
$$\frac{120}{480} = \frac{1}{4} = 25\%$$

### ✅ Answer = 25% profit

---

## ✏️ Question 5

CP = 900 | SP = 1080

### 🔍 Solution

**Profit:**
$$1080 - 900 = 180$$

**Divide:**
$$\frac{180}{900} = \frac{1}{5} = 20\%$$

### ✅ Answer = 20% profit

# 🥧 Topic 8 — Pie Chart

## 📌 Formula

$$\text{Value} = \frac{\text{Angle}}{360} \times \text{Total}$$

## ⚡ Shortcut

> **1° value = Total ÷ 360** → then multiply by the given angle.

---

## ✏️ Question 1

Total students = 720 | CSE angle = 90°

### 🔍 Solution

$$1° \text{ value} = \frac{720}{360} = 2$$
$$90 \times 2 = 180$$

### ✅ Answer = 180

---

## ✏️ Question 2

Total employees = 360 | HR = 60°

### 🔍 Solution

$$1° \text{ value} = \frac{360}{360} = 1$$
$$60 \times 1 = 60$$

### ✅ Answer = 60

---

## ✏️ Question 3

Total production = 1080 | Sector = 120°

### 🔍 Solution

$$1° \text{ value} = \frac{1080}{360} = 3$$
$$120 \times 3 = 360$$

### ✅ Answer = 360

---

## ✏️ Question 4

Total = 900 | Angle = 180°

### 🔍 Solution

$$180° = \text{half}$$
$$\frac{900}{2} = 450$$

### ✅ Answer = 450

---

## ✏️ Question 5

Total marks = 1440 | Math sector = 120°

### 🔍 Solution

$$120° = \frac{1}{3}$$
$$\frac{1440}{3} = 480$$

### ✅ Answer = 480
